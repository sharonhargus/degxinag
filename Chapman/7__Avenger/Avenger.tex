Unlike the other Chapman texts, which were dictated, this one was recorded on wax cylinders. Rufus (or Rufous) Morton helped Chapman with the transcription and translation of this text.

The recording of Edna Deacon took place at the home of Hannah Maillelle, who was also present during the recording.
