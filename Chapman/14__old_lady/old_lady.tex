\title{TR'AN TTHUXDI DHIDONH \newline The Little Old Lady Who Lived Alone \footnote{Kari's title was \em Tr'an Sughiluq Tthuxdi Dhidoninh. \em Chapman's title was `The Old Woman and the Singing Fish'. This text is erroneously listed in the table of contents of his book as being English-only.}}

\gll Tr'an soghluq dhido.
{old lady} dear {she lived}
\glt `There was a little old lady who lived (alone).'
\glend

\gll Ideyan' noghnik iɬt'et.
alone {she worked} always
\glt `She always worked by herself.'
\glend

\gll Sanh tux xiyiɬ tigitlcheth.
summer in then {she set net}
\glt `In summer she set a net.'
\glend

\gll Yitots'in' ɬegg xilong it'anh.
then fish many {she has}
\glt `And she caught a lot of fish.'
\glend

\gll Yitots'in' yit'oth ts'ixiyiɬ dodighiydilayh ts'ixiyiɬ yiyiɬggoyh.

\glt `She cut them, hung them up, and dried them.'
\glend

\gll Yitots'i tso ye yilayh.

\glt `And then she put them in the cache.'
\glend

\gll Gidingidz tso it'anh.

\glt `She had a grass cache.'
\glend

\gll {Iy tots'i} vav long it'anh.

\glt `Then she had plenty of food.'
\glend

\gll Sidaɬts'eyh ts'ixiyiɬ vav long it'anh ts'in'.
{she's happy} then food plenty {she has} 
\glt `She was happy, since she had plenty of food.'
\glend

\gll Xiyh che nonxididhit yitots'in' giɬvatr ts'ixiyiɬ.

\glend

\gll Gitth'in yan' iɬvatr hingo vav long it'anh ine'.

\glt `She cooked only bones, although she had plenty of food.'
\glend
