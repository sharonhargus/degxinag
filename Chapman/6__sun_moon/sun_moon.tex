\gll Xanegin longh yiɬ xiyq'at {xiyghun' ididiɬ,} qay yit xit'an yiɬ, daɬine' tr'igidiɬt'ix.

\glt `Many strangers came seeking her, as did many people of that village, but she refused to marry.'
\glend

\gll Yitots'in' xidigaɬ nuq'oɬdaɬin chilqay yiɬ qay xit'an  xixaghɬzrek.

\glend

%and kashime {when they come out of,} who {it may be} hair tied for {I will look,''} {she thought.}
\glt `Then when the men come out of the kashim I'll look to see who has feathers tied to his hair,'' she thought.'
