GAN A NINGIŁ’AN?
(What do you see?)

Raymond Dutchman

  1. hiddenGananingiɬ’an?Gananingiɬ’an?
    What do you see? What do you see?

  2. hiddenGiɬigg,teqa,toggdineg.
    One, two, three moose.

  3. hiddenDinegnitl-’anh.
    I see some moose.

  4. hiddenGananingiɬ’an?Gananingiɬ’an?
    What do you see? What do you see?

  5. hiddenGiɬigg,teqa,toggnoya’.
    One, two, three beaver.

  6. hiddenNoya’nitl-’anh.
    I see some beaver.

  7. hiddenGananingiɬ’an?Gananingiɬ’an?
    What do you see? What do you see?

  8. hiddenGiɬigg,teqa,toggnoghniy.
    One, two, three rabbits.

  9. hiddenNoghniynitl-’anh.
    I see some rabbits.
