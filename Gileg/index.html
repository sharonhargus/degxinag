Gileg is a book of English songs translated into Deg Xinag by Alta Jerue (Anvik dialect), working with Donna Miller.

The 1999 and previous versions were prepared by Donna Miller, working with Alta Jerue (Anvik dialect), and published by the Iditarod Area School District.

In 2006 audio recordings of Gileg were made with the following Deg Xinag speakers in Shageluk or Grayling, Alaska: James Dementi, Raymond Dutchman, Lucy Hamilton, and Edna Deacon.
